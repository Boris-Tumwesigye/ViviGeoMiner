ViviGeoMiner

Introduction

Modern mineral exploration combines geology, geophysics, remote sensing, geochemistry and advanced technology to investigate the Earth's mineral resources.

ViviGeoMiner aims to make geological knowledge and exploration technology more accessible, particularly in Uganda and South Sudan.

01

Geological Mapping

Geological mapping involves studying and recording rocks, geological structures, faults, folds and mineral occurrences in a particular area.

Field observations and geological maps help exploration geologists understand the geological history of an area and identify locations that deserve further investigation.

02

Magnetic Surveys

Magnetic surveys measure variations in the Earth's magnetic field caused by differences in the magnetic properties of underlying rocks.

Professional surveys use specialised magnetometers mounted on aircraft, vehicles or carried by field geologists.

These surveys can help investigate geological structures and rocks containing magnetic minerals such as magnetite.

03

Electrical Resistivity Surveys

Electrical resistivity methods investigate how strongly underground materials resist the flow of electrical current.

Geologists use specialised electrodes and instruments to investigate subsurface structures, groundwater conditions and geological formations.

04

Satellite Imagery and Remote Sensing

Satellite imagery provides information about the Earth's surface and can help identify geological features, alteration zones and landform patterns.

Multispectral and hyperspectral remote sensing can assist in mapping certain surface mineral signatures. Results require geological verification in the field.

05

Geochemical Exploration

Geochemical exploration involves collecting and analysing rock, soil, sediment or water samples to investigate the distribution of chemical elements.

Laboratory analysis can help identify element concentrations and geochemical patterns associated with potential mineralisation.

06

Modern Exploration Equipment

  • Professional magnetometers
  • GPS geological positioning devices
  • Portable X-ray fluorescence (pXRF) analysers
  • Electrical resistivity instruments
  • Geological sampling equipment
  • Geological mapping software

Technology and Responsible Exploration

No single technology can independently confirm an economically viable mineral deposit. Reliable exploration requires multiple geological investigations, appropriate sampling, laboratory testing and professional interpretation.

ViviGeoMiner's Vision

Our vision is to develop an accessible digital platform supporting mineral education, geological field observations and responsible mineral exploration in Africa.
