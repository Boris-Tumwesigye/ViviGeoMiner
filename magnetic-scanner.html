How it works

The magnetometer measures magnetic field components along three axes. The total field strength is calculated using:

√(X² + Y² + Z²)

Measurements are displayed in microtesla (µT).

Scientific limitations

Magnetic anomalies may have several geological or artificial causes. Nearby metal, electronic devices, phone orientation and calibration can affect readings. This tool cannot identify minerals or confirm an ore deposit. Professional geological surveys are required.
