Understanding Your Readings

The magnetometer measures the magnetic field around your phone. Readings can change due to nearby metal, electrical equipment, magnets and natural geological variations.

Move your phone slowly and compare readings at different locations, keeping its orientation consistent.

Important Scientific Notice

Magnetic readings do not identify minerals or prove the presence of an ore deposit. A phone magnetometer is a demonstration and preliminary observation tool. Professional geological surveys and laboratory analysis are needed to evaluate mineral deposits.
